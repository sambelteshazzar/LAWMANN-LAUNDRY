# Lawmann audit-001, 2026-09-29

Scope: the existing Next.js app (`src/`), assessed against antislop rules,
ui-ux-pro-max priorities, and competitor research (Rinse, Tide Cleaners,
JN Laundry Solutions, Smile Laundry GH). Owner perceived the site as
"unprofessional, no landing page, goes straight to login, too local".

## Framing: what this app is

The approved spec (docs/specs/2026-09-28-v1-core-design.md) defines this build
as the back-of-house system: PIN login for collector/counter/owner, students
receive SMS only. "No landing page, straight to login" is that design, not an
accident. Rinse and Tide Cleaners structure the same way: a public marketing
site (rinse.com, tidecleaners.com) is separate from the staff/customer app
(my.tidecleaners.com/accounts/login, /accounts/login). Lawmann has only the
second surface. The gap is a missing surface, not a broken one.

## Findings: internal app

| # | Finding | Rule | Priority | Reason |
|---|---------|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Domain root requires staff PIN; any visitor (student, partner, landlord) sees "Who is working?" | R-37 | HIGH | The product has no public face. This is the main driver of the "local" feel |
| 2 | No brand identity asset: no logo, generic system font stack, no brand typeface | R-20, R-06 | HIGH | Swap "Lawmann" for any name and the app looks identical. Utility styling is correct for a POS, but there is zero identity layer |
| 3 | No customer-facing surfaces at all: no services, no pricing, no how-it-works, no contact, no WhatsApp booking, no order tracking (spec defers tracking to phase 4) | R-37 | HIGH | Every competitor site leads with these |
| 4 | Login is the front door, not a corner link | R-37 | MEDIUM | Competitors tuck login into the nav corner |
| 5 | Root dashboard headline "Today" with no product name on mobile (SideNav hidden on md-) | R-20 | LOW | On a phone the screen never says who it belongs to |

## What the internal app does RIGHT (verified against rules)

- Money: integer pesewas, trigger-enforced laws, pending MoMo shown separately.
  No fake statistics (R-17 PASS), no fabricated claims (R-36 PASS).
- Mobile-first: 48px touch targets, 16px inputs (iOS zoom prevention), tabular
  figures (R-03 PASS).
- UI states: empty states on dashboard/arrears, error translation from triggers,
  loading via server rendering (R-27 PASS).
- Badges always labeled, never color alone (a11y). Focus rings present (R-32 PASS).
- No AI slop patterns: no gradients, no glassmorphism, no template layouts,
  no dead controls, real content everywhere.
- Bottom nav 5 tabs max, role-filtered (ui-ux-pro-max nav rules PASS).

## Findings: competitor research (how the professional ones look)

**Rinse (US, premium, rinse.com)**
- Public marketing site; staff/customer login is a nav-corner link
- Hero: "We'll take the laundry. You take the time." The CTA is a pickup
  widget ("Pickup Tonight / Add address"), not a generic button
- Press logos, Wash & Fold + Dry Cleaning service sections, first-pickup
  expectations, guarantee, pricing page, locations, FAQ
- Note for our copy: Rinse uses "Effortless", which antislop bans as a
  buzzword; their structure is worth adapting, not their wording

**Tide Cleaners (US, tidecleaners.com)**
- "Find a Location" + "Try Delivery" dual CTA
- Services grid incl. CAMPUS touchpoint (directly relevant: Lawmann serves
  students), lockers, pickup & delivery, business
- Login lives at my.tidecleaners.com, linked from nav

**JN Laundry Solutions (Accra, Madina, near UPSA & UG Legon: the closest comp)**
- Full public site: Home, Services, Pricing, About, Blog, FAQ, Track Order,
  Book Now
- "From 17 GHS/kg" and student discounts in meta/copy; delivery areas listed
  by name (Madina, Adentan, East Legon, Legon, Haatso...)
- How it works: 4 steps (Contact -> Pick up -> Clean -> Deliver)
- WhatsApp chat button, tel: links, mobile money mention, business hours
- SEO landing pages per area ("Laundry Near Legon", "Laundry Near UPSA")

**Smile Laundry GH (Accra)**
- Services incl. carpet, shoe, curtain cleaning; Order Online; 4-step process
  (Order -> Pickup -> Process -> Deliver); phone + WhatsApp CTAs
- Cautionary: template leftovers in their footer (Melbourne FL address,
  Latin filler text). What NOT to do

**Common Ghana pattern**
- WhatsApp-first booking, visible phone numbers, area lists, mobile money,
  student pricing. The professional local sites are not "global clones";
  they are global structure with local specifics.

## Recommended direction (needs owner approval before any build)

1. Keep the internal app as-is. It is the strongest part of the product.
2. Add a public surface: landing with a specific CTA (WhatsApp pickup or
   campus pickup), real pricing from the band table, how it works, areas
   served (the 17 hostels in the seed are real data), student value prop,
   contact. No fake testimonials, no invented numbers (R-17/R-18).
3. Staff login becomes a small footer/nav-corner link on the public surface.
4. Later, per the spec: customer tracking page (phase 4), reachable from the
   SMS links students already receive.

Open question for the owner (one at a time, per brainstorming):
- What is the public site's primary job: get students to book, or establish
  credibility with partners/investors?
